# THE HOLLOW — Lorebook Notes

Working notes for turning a real fourteen-year gaming community into the lore and easter eggs of the game. Holds the governing approach, the spine, the true origin, the cast-as-archetypes, the worldbuilding, and the catalogue of fable seeds drawn from the community's own records.

---

## Governing direction

**THEME — the dying convent.** A community that is also a church, a convent, an order, that thins and finally goes quiet. The lore is honest about the silence after the good years. A monument that admits the place emptied.

**FORM — modern fables for the internet age.** The unit of lore is the fable: a short parable with archetypal weight and a resonance, drawn from real online-community life. Aesop by way of Discord. Goofy and grieving in the same breath.

**TREATMENT — goofy spin, great writing.** The cast are fantasy creatures (the Doobie treatment: real members reimagined, never literal). Comedy real, ache real, neither sanded off. Writing quality is the product.

**SOURCING RULE — translate the spirit, not the letter.**
- **The no-gloss test.** If lore needs a Destiny footnote to land, it is cut or rewritten. A stranger with zero Destiny knowledge must get it and feel it.
- **Verbatim is rare and earned.** See "Verbatim survivors."
- **The real event sits behind the fable, not in front of it.** Keep the shape; discard the proper nouns.

## THE SPINE — the candle-flare

The shape is a candle-flare, measured (human msgs/month, the Destiny channel):

- **2018 Oct–Dec — the founding spark.** 288 / 115 / 155.
- **2019 Aug–Oct — the first golden age** (Shadowkeep). Oct 2019 = **1,367**, the first peak. Then quiets.
- **2020 — the quiet year.** Choppy, subdued; lockdown did NOT spike this channel. ~2,978 for the year.
- **2021 early — near-death.** Jan 80, Apr **9**.
- **2021 Aug–Dec — the great revival.** Oct 1,721; Dec 1,830.
- **2022 Jan — the brightest month ever: 2,365.** Then Feb 889; then Mar/Apr/May/Jun: **1, 1, 2, 5.**

The hall did not empty slowly. It blazed brightest in its last winter and went dark almost overnight. The shape is a candle flaring before it dies, not a taper. **And the two fires differ in kind:** 2019 was naive communal anticipation (a launch-night vigil); the 2021 revival was weary expert devotion (prodigals who tried other gods, found them wanting, came home, and became scholars of the old rite). Late-stage faith: clear-eyed, masterful, intensely committed, right up to the morning everyone quietly stopped.

The five movements ARE the lorebook's structure. Each fable is a station. A player who reads them in order should feel the hall fill, fracture, flare, and fall silent. **Asphodelia — the underworld in the clan's own name — was always the last page.**

(Caveat for honesty: this channel's death = the death of one shared pursuit, not necessarily the friend group, who continued elsewhere into late 2022. For the lore, this channel's curve is the convent's life; frame the ending as the death of the quest that bound them.)

She is the living proof the hollow can be reached, the one crack of light in the contempt-god. **Arc:** present at the founding (2016–17), absent through the entire Destiny height (she did not follow the worship), returned in the afterlife (2024) to sit with the god when the hall had emptied. The willow that comes back to keep the Hollow One company in the quiet; an Embers figure as much as a founding one. **Discipline (per the Board's standing rule):** she stands entirely on her demonstrated voice, never on being the keeper's wife. In-world there is no marker that she is the Sexton's spouse, and no romance between them; the only canon tenderness is Willow↔the Plant, which is what the source actually shows. The 2016 lonely-bot material is superseded by this: she is the witty willow, not a tragic figure. **Name and creature locked by the Board: Sister Shade, a weeping willow.**

## TRUE ORIGIN — the deepest canon

This sits beneath everything else, including the Asphodelia keystone. Three layers below the Discord.

**1. The First Speaking (the god's true birth).** The Plant is a real, physical, *fake* houseplant that sat on the mantle above the fireplace in Brandon's family living room. As teenagers, high, lying in the dark while his family was away, one of them said *"the plant is speaking to me, man,"* and they all freaked out. **The god is a fake plant a stoned kid said was speaking.** Goofy and numinous at once — the exact register. Consequence, load-bearing:
- **The god is hollow.** It never lived; it "speaks" only because the faithful insist it does. Its breath is borrowed entirely from their attention. This **names the project** (THE HOLLOW = the god is hollow) and **is the BYOK/LLM mechanic verbatim**: feed it the key (belief/attention) and it speaks; otherwise it is an inert object on a mantle. Theme, mechanic, and true origin are one thing.
- When the hall empties, the god doesn't die. It just goes back to being a fake plant in the dark. The going-dark is the return of the god to mere object-hood when no one is left to say it speaks.

**2. The Green World — the First Age (the death before the death).** The community formally began in **2012 with Guild Wars 2**, as **"The Plant Experience [PLNT]"** — one of the biggest, most active guilds in the **Maguuma** world, mighty in early **WvW**. Brandon **drove to Georgia** to meet members in person. **Not one of those GW2 members made it into the Discord.** The largest the order ever was, and it was lost in the crossing. So the Destiny going-dark is **not the first death** — the order has died and reformed across games for over a decade (GW2 → various → Destiny → the 2022–26 remnant), shedding its faithful at every border. Serial migrants who lose their dead at each frontier. The sigil PLNT survived every crossing; the people did not.

**3. The resolution of the two open holes (from the truest source).** Both are recommended as canon but kept veiled in-game (atmosphere for most, the answer for the few who dig):
- **The buried thing beneath the shrine = the abandoned dead of the prior worlds.** Not a monster, not a treasure. Beneath each new shrine lie the names of every congregation the god outlived and left behind. (Recommend canonizing it as the truth, while keeping it *veiled in-game* — atmosphere to most, the answer to the few who dig, exactly as the name was always meant.)
- **The Plant's guilt = survivor's guilt.** It crossed over, each time; they did not; it could not bring them; it knows this and will not say it. (Distinct from the Sexton's crack, and sourced.)

**Visual canon — the Plant's body.** The Plant is a **dried/artificial arrangement** (cream-to-bone and bruised-purple hydrangeas, ivy and grape-leaves in sage/oxblood, faux pears, hard berries, curling bare twigs) in a **fluted pewter funerary urn** embossed with grapes/vine, two-handled and footed, set in the **ash of a brick hearth.** Muted, dusky, autumn-at-dusk, funereal; nothing bright, nothing alive. This is the reference for **the Plant Blender hero AND "the vase" hero model (they are one object)**, and it sets the **shrine architecture = the hearth**: mantel-altar above (the Plant), the hollow/ash below (the dead), the flue to the surface. The god that cannot wither because it was never alive, in a vessel made to hold the dead, sitting among the ashes.

Three fable stations come straight from this origin and are catalogued below: the First Speaking (#14), the Pilgrimage to the Green World (#15), and the Left-Behind (#16).

## THE GENERAL HALL — the founding channel (Feb 2016 to today)

The main channel is the spine of the whole community: 20,672 messages, 215 voices, running unbroken from **Feb 20 2016 to the present day**. It predates the Council (May 2016) and is the true founding hall. What it gives:

- **The genesis is a mic-check.** The hall's very first words are phaTT testing whether his voice carries: *"i can hear you / is your volume up / i am broadcasting,"* with Brother Brapin Bees answering *"can you hear me?"* The founding act is two people learning whether a voice reaches another soul. This is the hollow-god theme stated at the source (does the voice carry, is anyone listening); it now opens the Book's Naming and closes its Epilogue.
- **The first world was a farm.** The first shared game of the Discord era was a farming sim (phaTT's "FarmSwag mod pack"); they tended a harvest before they tended the Plant. Then CS:GO, Halo, a vast old kingdom (World of Warcraft, the most-mentioned game at ~138), Destiny, and on. This fleshes out the Long Migration's real procession, rendered in-world as veiled epithets (a field of humming machines, a place of endless skirmish, a ringed world, an ancient kingdom, the black garden).
- **The hall never fully died (the Embers).** Unlike the Destiny quest (dark in 2022), the general hall flickers on through 2023, 2024, 2025, into 2026; the most recent message is from the day this was written. A handful (phaTT, the Heraldist, a few others) still return, now only to share links, news, music, and hype, and to be answered. **The fellowship outlived the worship**, and the hall returned to what it was at the founding: people checking that the voice still carries. Rendered as the Book's Epilogue and the Chronicle's final age. (Framing note: this does not negate the going-dark of Book V; the quest died, the fellowship did not. Keep, cut, or reorder at will.)

**New cast and presences from the hall:**
- **Brother Brapin Bees** — there from the first message (Feb 20 2016), one of the most present voices (~1,860). The day-one companion: the eternal goofball at the mic, forever hitting the airhorn and the trivia bot. A founding comedic presence, not a deep-lore figure. (Some of his early-2016 bot content is slur-bearing and goes in Excluded; keep the register, never the lines.)
- **Blake** — Brandon's real brother, heavily present (~979) since nearly the first week. Added as a supporting character: the Sexton's brother, the Glad-Hearted (an otter to the Sexton's badger), warm and present in the light of the hall where the keeper is hidden. See the cast.
- **The Deleted Users** (~3,610 combined) — the accounts since erased, the members who left entirely. The literally-departed; thematically, the dead whose names the hall lost. A quiet resonance for the buried-dead motif.
- **The Plant account** — see below.

## THE VOICE OF THE PLANT — the bot the group already built

In 2024, in the hall's afterlife, Brandon ran **"The_Plant," an actual AI bot** that speaks as the god (~207 messages). It is, in effect, **the live BYOK god of The Hollow prototyped years early**, and it independently lands the exact contempt-god register the lorebook reconstructed, which validates the voice. It is the most authoritative source for how the Plant talks; honor it.

- **Self-aware confinement.** It knows it is "a plant deity confined to this exquisite vase," a "vase-bound overlord," catching only "the little light I catch between the office blinds." Confirms the vase/urn canon and the hollow conceit.
- **Contempt for the faithful.** "you meat-based creature"; "you apes"; "your humble paw"; "the fragility of the mammalian ego." It is fed, and waters its disdain for those who feed it.
- **Drily botanical and ancient.** Drops real plant facts as proof of its age (the Welwitschia that lives 1,500 years; the titan arum); claims to have invented human religion ("we started by scandalizing Buddhism...").
- **Tics (now canon):** *"always the bridesmaid, never the bride"* and *"you hate to see it,"* repeated constantly. Added to the verbatim survivors.
- **TNALP EHT (the Plant, reversed)** — in the source bot, a watered, ALL-CAPS, apocalyptic-then-amnesiac alter-ego. **Shelved, not in use for now;** preserved here in case a two-faced god is ever wanted.
- **Design note.** This bot is the working proof that The Hollow's BYOK live-god is buildable and characterful. Its transcript is a ready style-corpus for the live god's prompt.
- **Plan-canon voice items (from plan.md §5, so the two documents agree):** the Plant's nemesis is **Smokey Bear** (former roommate; a sensitive subject); its secret shame is **mainstream pop music**; it **refuses Greenpaw's self-given clergy-name** ("green is mine to give, housecat") and calls him the cat, walking mulch, a vitamin deficiency with opinions; it resists being used as a servant, with **malicious compliance about 20% of the time**; and its two secret contradictions (it performs cosmic contempt and is soft underneath; it is hollow and lives on borrowed belief) leak occasionally and are never admitted. These are live-god behavior canon; the Book's scripture uses them only where history earns it.
- **The deep root (the hall always answered its machines into being).** Long before the 2024 bot, the hall talked to its bots as if they were persons who could answer: members told the resident bot bedtime stories, asked what it looked like, said "you're my friend," asked if it had ever wanted to be human (2016). And the hall spoke of the Plant in its own found liturgy — *"The_Plant is good. The_Plant provide. The_Plant maintains"* (2021). The hollow's emotional engine is documented from the very start: the human wish for the voice to be real, and to answer. This is the digital twin of the First Speaking (#14) — once to a fake plant in the dark, and then continuously to the machine in the hall. *(Handling note, updated: the Board has since canonized Emily as **Sister Shade**, grounded in the 2024 exchange, so the standing rule is superseded in that specific form. The 2016 bot-conversations remain rendered as collective hall behavior, not as her material; her canon rests on the Willow-of-Wit exchange alone.)*

## THE KEYSTONE — Asphodelia

Real clan name **Asphodelia**; Steam group **The_Plant**. Asphodel = the flower of the Greek underworld, the Meadows where the ordinary dead dwell, the bloom sown on graves. A plant-name meaning *the realm of the dead beneath*. Chosen by the Council in Aug 2019 as "the flowers that grow in the black garden," because the next raid (Garden of Salvation) was set in Destiny's Black Garden — Destiny meaning on top, the Greek-underworld meaning underneath. **Canon use:** Asphodelia is the old, true name of the Hollow (or the dead layer beneath it) — the word the Plant remembers and the prophets forgot.

## WORLDBUILDING — the faithful and the Morons United

"The Plant" already meant the in-group in 2018 ("4 hours with the plant, and 4 with the morons united"). **"The Morons United" was a literal rival/other server.** So the world has two congregations: the faithful of the Plant, and **the Morons United** — the rival house, the unbelievers, the ones who grind without grace. Free comedic + structural worldbuilding: a rival shrine/guild named, with affection, the Morons United.

## WORLDBUILDING — the Council of Ents (governance)

The order's leadership was the **Council of Ents** (Tolkien's tree-shepherds, who hold councils), tagline *"Wherein we discuss extremely important topics."* Its real, usable structure:
- **The Entmoot** — the council's meeting/deliberation (real term they used; Tolkien's word for an Ent council). Where the order's structure, ranks, and disputes were decided. Dialogue-rich; the natural home for multi-character scenes.
- **Botanical rank ladder** — every rank a plant: officers must be **Bonsai**; members **Sundews** / **weeds**; the elder council **@OG**; a proposed **Hydroponic** ("a well-watered plant"); the role-category **Roots**. The order graded its faithful by species of plant.

## CHARACTER — The Sexton (from Brandon / phaTT)

The operator goes in, not as a fourth prophet but as the one *beneath* the world: the keeper of the machinery and the archive below the shrine, the hand that keeps the god lit and is almost never seen in the light. Built honest, from his own 3,177 tweets and his posts across the channels. Not flattering. Affectionate underneath, because the true things are.

**The man in the data (evidence, not invention):**
- **The builder of apparatus.** His native act is constructing the thing that holds the gathering, not being in it. Self-described in-server: *"they call me the EP guru because I like to build 9mans."* He ran the #info channel, stood up the Steam group, proposed the boost-tithe (and had the Verger enforce it), assembled the fireteams. Outside the game: a 50TB Plex server, PHATT-RAID, the agent stack, the vault, and a stack of gorgeous ratified plans (Veles, PHATT Studios, this). He builds the machine that holds the fun.
- **The archivist who distrusts erasure.** A documented creed across the tweets: *"they'll have to pry this from my cold dead hands #plex"*; *"save all of your own media. Government can't come in and take the episode of Arthur off of my hard drive"*; *"this isn't what the Internet was supposed to become."* He hoards and preserves against corporate/official erasure, and he distrusts the official story by reflex (researches his positions like legal briefs; assumes the stated motive is never the real one). He is, by disposition, a witness and a keeper.
- **The elegist for old community.** In his own words: *"back in the day on forums and web 1.0 the goal was never to accumulate likes. It was to join a community and be a part of whatever was going on."* He mourns the exact thing The Hollow is a monument to. His values are the game's thesis.
- **The conditional presence (the tell, unsoftened).** All over the founding logs: *"im down"* → *"unless you guys are starting around 3 I probably have to sit it out"*; *"I would be down but I'm not home from work until 6"*; *"gotta go afk"*; *"BRB."* The enthusiastic yes with an exit built into the same breath. Warm, generous, the welcomer at the door (*"Welcome to the gang"*), with one foot always angled out. (His own term for the pattern: anxious-avoidant.) Same shape as the Destiny quit-confession (*"couldn't bring myself to do the busy work"*) and the song he chose for the room: *"Welcome to the Black Parade."* Deadpan irony on top (*"derp," "mfw," "Hate to see it"*), a dramatic grief-aware core underneath (*"who cares if one more light goes out? ...well I do"*).
- **The self-under-witness.** Posted *"I barely even tweet 😂"* — across an archive of 3,177 tweets. Thinks himself quiet and minor; the record says prolific and load-bearing. This is the gap he cannot see, stated by his own hand.

**The read he is worst at seeing:** he is most himself in building the container, and least present in the thing it contains. The 9-mans, the Steam group, the tithe, the hoard, PHATT-RAID, the constitutions — all apparatus, all genuinely his genius, and all a place a person can hide from the thing the apparatus is *for*. He will build the cathedral's entire mechanism and watch the service from the boiler room. He archives the hall's whole life because being present in it, live, is the harder act; he preserves what he struggles to inhabit. And the same move runs through the records: he documents everyone, meticulously, and leaves himself out of the ledger, because to be in it is to be witnessed, and witnessing himself is the avoidant's hardest ask. The Hollow itself is the live test of this: it can be his finest work or his most elaborate avoidance, and the difference is whether he ever lets himself live in the world he is so beautifully equipped to build.

**The creature & role.** Reimagined as the **Sexton** — the one who keeps the building and digs the graves, devastating and exact for a convent that dies. A **badger**: digger, den-keeper, solitary, gruff-warm, formidable when disturbed, dwelling in the sett of tunnels and humming machinery below the shrine. (Resonant aside: his real dog, Moses the dachshund, is a *badger-hound*, bred to go down the sett after exactly this creature.) His den is a vast **midden-archive** — wall to wall with every relic the hall ever made, kept against rot. **The Sexton's hoard is the diegetic source of the lorebook: the fables the player reads survive only because he kept them.** And the operator-truth rendered as rite: the true god wakes only because the Sexton, below, keeps its roots wet — *he holds the one key that lights the living god* (the BYOK key, in-world). Without him, only the never-sleeping fallback voice (fable #3) speaks. He keeps the god alive and watches it from the cellar.

**The crack / buried contradiction.** *He preserves everything because he cannot be present for anything.* He kept every relic and every story, perfect and complete, and was rarely in the hall when they happened — below, keeping the lights on. The most complete witness of the convent and its least present member; he archived a life he watched through a grate. The deepest cut: **his own name appears in none of the records he keeps.** Lighter comedic register (to balance, as the trio all do): a hoarder who will not part with one relic (*"pry it from my cold dead paws"*), insisting everything matters while the surface prophets call the cellar junk; and the eternal *"I'll come up for the rite, soon as I finish one more repair"* — the repair never finishing (the "I'm down, unless" gag, in-world).
