Zone placement tool (dev only)

WASD fly, hold right mouse to look, Q/E or Space/Shift up/down, wheel = speed. Pick a piece, left-click the ground to place, R rotates 15 deg (Shift+R back), Esc disarms. Each row's x removes that placement; clear session resets without a reload. Nothing is saved: copy the literal into the zone content module.
